Scarlet Witch

Scarlet Witch, also known as Wanda Maximoff, is one of the most powerful characters in the Marvel Universe. She possesses extraordinary magical abilities, including reality manipulation, telekinesis, and energy projection. Wanda is a complex character whose story is shaped by both her immense power and the challenges she faces. Her journey explores themes of loss, love, identity, and the struggle to control her abilities. She is known for being both a powerful superhero and a deeply emotional character.
