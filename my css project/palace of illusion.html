📖 The Palace of Illusions

⭐ A Mythological Retelling ⭐

About the Book

The Palace of Illusions is a novel by Chitra Banerjee Divakaruni. The story is a retelling of the Indian epic, the Mahabharata, from the perspective of Draupadi.

About Draupadi

Draupadi is the central character of the novel. Through her eyes, we see the events of the Mahabharata, her relationships, her struggles, and her desire to have control over her own destiny.

Why I Like This Book

What makes this book interesting is that it gives a different perspective on a story that many people already know. It focuses on Draupadi's thoughts, emotions, ambitions, and experiences.

"A story of power, destiny, love, and the search for one's own identity."
