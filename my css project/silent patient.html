About the Story

Alicia Berenson is a famous painter whose life appears perfect until her husband is found dead. Alicia is accused of the murder—and then she stops speaking completely.

She never explains what happened. Not a single word. Her silence turns the case into an obsession for those trying to discover the truth.

Theo Faber, a psychotherapist, becomes determined to uncover what happened on the night of the murder and why Alicia has chosen silence.

Characters
