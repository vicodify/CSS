The Story

Alicia Berenson appears to have a perfect life. She is a successful painter, married to a famous fashion photographer, and living in a beautiful London home.

Until one evening, her husband is found dead.

Alicia is accused of the murder. But instead of explaining what happened, she stops speaking completely.

Not one word.

"Her silence became the mystery."

THE SILENT PATIENT

About The Book

The Silent Patient is a psychological thriller written by Alex Michaelides. The story explores obsession, trauma, relationships and the secrets people keep hidden.

Theo Faber, a psychotherapist, becomes fascinated by Alicia's case. He believes that if he can make her speak, he may finally understand what happened on the night of the murder.

But the deeper Theo looks into Alicia's past, the more complicated the mystery becomes.
